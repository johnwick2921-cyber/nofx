## ONE-PAGE SUMMARY

**Verdict on the level subsystem.** The map is well built and badly measured. The detectors are clean, deterministic and cheap; the seating machinery has more guarantees than a desk of six would ever need. But the one instrument that was supposed to tell the owner which levels matter — the live D1′ touch corpus — is contaminated three ways in code that shipped 36 hours ago, and every number anyone has quoted from it (48.8 % first-touch hold, RTH-L breaking 68 %, the "retire six kinds" list) is an artifact of that contamination, not a property of the tape. When I re-run the same detector honestly over the full persisted tape, **no kind separates from a random-level null of 0.506, in either direction** — including RTH-L, which holds 0.628, not breaks 0.68. The prior day's high and low, the two anchors every trader on NQ watches, are computed from a calendar-day bucket read off a 33-hour ring, and were wrong by 37–218 points on five of eight days I could check. And the seating is not a filter: 92 % of seats are grade A, twelve of twelve seats fill every read, and a fifth of the seats sit more than 100 points from price where they are reached one time in fifteen. **Nothing in this section is ready to be traded as a "level edge"; the honest work is to fix the instrument and let the tape grow.**

**The three biggest problems.**

1. **The live touch corpus cannot be used, and it is already being used [A].** `trader/detector_record.go:57` scans the whole 2,000-bar void scope (~33 h, `kernel/svp.go:46-47`) for every seated level on every planner read, but the idempotency watermark (`store/touch_outcomes.go:254-266`) and the ordinal (`:73-84`) are scoped to `opened_at_ms >= <current CME session-day start>`. So every episode that opened before 17:00 CT the prior evening is re-recorded, with ordinal 1, on every read. **677 rows are 423 distinct episodes; RTH-L's 140 rows are 14 episodes of one price copied up to 11 times** (`q06`). Worse, nothing cuts the scan at the level's formation time (`DetectedLevel.FormedAtMs`, `kernel/levels.go:89`, is never read by the recorder): **all 14 RTH-L episodes opened before 15:00 CT 2026-09-03, i.e. before 29199.25 was the prior RTH low at all** (`q10`, "the 14 distinct RTH-L episodes"). Dynamic lines (VWAP, swings, OR) are scanned at today's price over yesterday's bars. And `BuildCandidatePool` (`kernel/detector_recorder.go:67-90`) writes `score 0.0, grade ''` for every cut row although the graded pool was in hand — a fabricated zero (canon "no fabricated values"). `research-conformance.md:243-300` and `veteran-review.md:82-103` both computed rates from this table.
2. **PDH/PDL/PDC are the wrong numbers three separate ways [A].** (a) `kernel/levels_multiday.go:146-160` buckets by CT *calendar* day, so at the 08:00 NY read "yesterday" includes 17:00–23:59 of the current Globex session: on 08-27 the engine's PDH 29655.75 was 218.5 pts above the session-day PDH 29437.25 — it was the overnight high (`q11_replay.txt`, "calendar-day vs session-day"); calendar ≠ session on 5 of 8 measurable days (−182.75, +78.75, +218.5/+37.5, +80.5, +42.5). (b) At the 16:30 CT ASIA read the prior *calendar* day is two days back and the RTH that closed 90 minutes earlier is excluded (`mostRecentPriorDay`, `:229-237`, `k < todayCal`): ASIA plans for 08-24, 08-25 and 08-27 carry D-2's extremes (`q14`). (c) The bucket is built from the 2,000-bar ring, and the 900-bar coverage guard admits a bucket missing up to ~9 h: at the 09-02 13:58 CT read the engine emitted PDH 29317.25 (`level_state`, `q15`) while calendar 09-01's high was 29571 printed at 00:xx CT, outside the ring; that number was then carried into the 09-02 ASIA plan as "PDH".
3. **Seating does not rank and the grade does not gate [A].** Across all 254 plans (2,391 seated rows) grades are A 1,920 (80.3 %) / B 152 / C 319 (`q12a`); deduplicated to one row per level-day, **A = 906 of 985 (92 %)**. Twelve of twelve seats fill in 11 of 12 recorded reads (`q04`). 217 of 985 seated levels (22 %) sat >100 pts from price at seat and were reached 44.7 % (<200) and 6.6 % (200+) of the time (`q12b`, distance buckets). Downstream, nothing consumes level grade: not `trader/entry_gate.go`, `kernel/entry_law.go`, `kernel/plan_confirm.go`, `kernel/arm_kind.go` nor `trader/armed_executor.go` (grep, zero hits); grade changes only seat order, the `min_grade` cut (which Tier-1 kinds are exempt from, `levels_score.go:70-84`) and prompt text.

**The three biggest opportunities.**

1. **Fix the instrument, then let time do the work [A/T].** Three code changes (formation-time scan start, full-window watermark, persisted real scores for cut rows) make `touch_outcomes` and `candidate_pool` the tables the 09-03 wave designed. The replay's pre-registered n ≥ 200 per-kind floor is the right bar; nothing in this section clears it and nothing will for weeks. Every hour spent re-weighting `levels_score.go` before that is fitting noise — the 09-02 replay said so and I agree with it.
2. **Two hypotheses worth pre-registering [T, no verdict].** (a) A lost prior low re-tested *from below* holds as resistance 0.680 [0.484, 0.828] (n=25, RTH-L) and 0.680 [0.484, 0.828] (n=25, PDL) on my replay — the classic flip, and the opposite of a fade-long. (b) EQH first touch holds 0.711 [0.566, 0.823] (n=45) on the plan-forward tape while EQL holds 0.473 (n=55). Both are n-tiny, both overlap 0.5 at the bottom, both are the kind of asymmetry I would fund a measurement for.
3. **Seat by reachability, not by decoration [T/I].** Seated levels on 09-04 were touched 52.5 % of the time vs 16.0 % for the cut ones (`q12`), and the difference is distance (134 vs 218 pts), not grade. A ±0.5×dATR seat band (this trader runs 1.0), zones as confluence only, an 8-seat executor table with the 12-seat planner pool, and the two redundant VWAP-family lines demoted to display would put roughly a third more of the map inside the day's reach.

## 2 · WHICH KINDS I WOULD SEAT, WHICH THE TAPE SEATS, AND WHOM I TRUST

**From experience [I].** On NQ the levels that matter are the ones other people's orders are parked at, and those are boringly conventional: the prior *Globex session* high/low/close (17:00→16:00, not a calendar day), the overnight high/low (as the first liquidity of the RTH day), the opening range (5 and 15/30 min), the initial balance, the session VWAP, the prior settlement, and the prior day's value area. Round hundreds are a tie-break, never a seat. Zones (OB/FVG/S/D) are where I *expect* a reaction to begin, not where I rest an order; equal highs/lows are where I expect a stop run to end. I would never seat more than the day can reach.

**What the tape says [T].** Pooled by family on the replay: prior-day 0.570 [0.517, 0.622] (n=335), open 0.534 [0.453, 0.613] (n=146), profile 0.509 (n=214), VWAP family 0.503 (n=189), round 0.514 (n=1,155), **overnight 0.439 [0.349, 0.534] (n=107)**. No session effect: NY 0.513, ASIA 0.520, LONDON 0.537 — but ASIA's ambiguity is 37.2 % against NY's 15.1 %, because the band is a session-wide constant (see §5, time-of-day).

**The one cell that made me sit up [T, n=25, no verdict].** Split by the side price *came from*: RTH-L approached from **below** (price already under the prior low, rallying back into it) holds 17/8 → 0.680 [0.484, 0.828]; from above (the classic fade-long) 10/8 → 0.556 [0.337, 0.754].

## ONE-PAGE SUMMARY

**Verdict for section 7: the planner prompt is a well-instrumented checklist that has stopped being a checklist, and the executor prompt is a 10,800-token document sent every two minutes to a model that, under `plan_mode=strict`, is not allowed to do the one thing the document keeps offering it.** The good work is real and I will name it: the class-38 contract guard (19 restrictions, verified at boot), the class-45 feed-forward of the validator's own verdicts, the boot-verified executor golden, the `pnl_corrected` track record. What is wrong is not the engineering discipline. It is that nobody has read either prompt end-to-end as the model reads it since the waves started landing, and the reject record shows the model paying for that.

**The three biggest problems (measured, ids named):**

1. **A new three-attempt death loop appeared on 09-04 and the prior review's window closed one hour before it.** Of the 16 rejects on 09-04, 14 (87.5 %, Wilson [64.0, 96.5]) are one chain repeated three times at ONL 29481.50 (ids 124→125→126, 127→128→129, 130→131→132, at 09:37, 10:00, 10:30 CT): attempt 1 "the tape shows NO confirming close beyond 29481.50 yet … (or set entry_mode=immediate …)" → attempt 2 the model obeys that hint and is refused "arm requires entry_mode=pullback" → attempt 3 it moves the play and is refused "measured displacement 0.00 pts". Three NY wake re-reads were lost (log: `🗓️ wake re-read failed for 2026-09-04 NY (benign — active plan kept)` ×3). Two causes, both in the prompt's own feed-forward: the displacement table says "29481.50 ONL — 238.50 pts **up** · at or above the floor — **authorable**" without saying *authorable as what* (`kernel/displacement_feeds_forward.go:111-113`), and the BD_MIN_CLOSES hint names an option (`entry_mode=immediate`) that the arm validator refuses two lines later (`kernel/breakdown_continue.go:261` vs `:270`). Class 34's law — "a hint is an instruction — instructions must be checkable" — is broken by the hint the class-45 wave itself ships. And each attempt-1 wake re-read starts blind: 0 of 30 attempt-1 prompts carry any prior read's corrections (`q24`), so the 10:00 read repeated the 09:37 death exactly.
2. **The executor prompt is not "the right size" — it is the same size as the planner prompt and it is sent 14× more often.** Live record 37768 (09-04 13:27 CT): system 11,121 chars / 3,318 tokens + input 16,448 chars / 8,504 tokens = **27,569 chars / 11,822 o200k tokens**; the provider billed 10,866 average over 373 executor calls that day (range 1,462–11,886). Of those 11,822 tokens, ≈1,450 are instruction (12 %) and the rest is bars, indicator arrays and the plan; 93 % of the answers are `wait`. And the 12 % that is instruction still tells the model "a valid off-plan setup may still be traded (cite "off-plan")" with an `open_long` worked example (`kernel/plan_render.go:156`, `engine_prompt_futures.go:252`) under a mode that refuses every decision-path market entry at `trader/entry_gate.go:160-163`: **13 of the 14 open proposals on 09-03 were refused "strict"** (20:35–21:12 CT, ids 37304–37322; 92.9 % [68.5, 98.7]), each after a 60–124 s AI call, and since 21:12 CT 09-03 the executor has proposed nothing at all (0 opens in 374 records on 09-04). The word "strict" appears in the executor prompt only as "Entry Standards (Strict)" and "Strictly Follow". The prior review saw this defect in the golden; the store shows it costing calls.
3. **The prompt still asserts market beliefs the tape refutes, in the same voice as the Go-computed facts.** "Conviction: down on Monday, up Thursday/Friday" (`planner_prompt.go:656`, in 47 of 47 stored full prompts): on 1,898 stored MNQ daily session bars (2019-05 → 2026-09) **Monday closes above its open 62.4 % of the time [57.4, 67.2], n=378 — the strongest up-day of the week**; Thursday 52.8 % [47.7, 57.7], Friday 50.7 % [45.6, 55.7] (`q20`, session-open-stamp corrected — see Surprises). "reject-based setups are best in NY RTH (75 % win, +665 this week)" (`:737`): `/api/expectancy` reject n=31, 14 wins, **45.2 % [29.2, 62.2]**, +586, status FAILS. "acceptance … 0 % win evidence" (`:660`): n=6, 1 win, 16.7 % [3.0, 56.4], +4.6. None of the three carries a label, an n, or a date; all three are read 47 times a week.

**The three biggest opportunities:**

1. **Cut ≈3,700 tokens (34 %) from the planner prompt without losing a rule the machine enforces:** the 6-timeframe indicator array dump (`## Indicators (executor mirror …)`, `planner_prompt.go:450`: 2,553 tokens, 23.3 % of the prompt — twenty 4-decimal EMA/RSI values per timeframe that a plan-writer with candle tables cannot use), the FVG demand block (≈1,100 tokens for a condition that is `shadow` and whose candidate list was empty in **47 of 47** stored reads), and the digest chain's operational noise (580 tokens, `"errors today: 234 (types: guardrail_would_trip, no_balance_frame)"` printed twice per day).
2. **Turn the fed-forward verdicts into decisions the model can copy:** per level, print the one condition that IS authorable there and in which direction ("29481.50 ONL — breakup 238.5 pts, authorable as `breakup_continue` long; `breakdown_continue` short would be refused: no confirming close"). The rejects show the model copying the machine's verdicts faithfully — it needs them to be complete. Same for the BD_MIN_CLOSES hint: name both legal exits ("drop the arm and set immediate, or keep the arm and author `reject` at the level").
3. **Give the executor the truth about its authority and the floor** — one rendered line: "plan_mode=strict: you cannot open. Your job is to grade the plan's scenarios, manage an open position, and say `no breakdown scenario authored` when the plan is missing one." That single change would let the owner cut the executor to its 1,450 instruction tokens plus the plan block plus the last 20 bars, and pay for ~4,000 tokens a cycle instead of 10,800. Then wire `IncBreakdownGapNoted` — the phrase the prompt promises "the system counts" was written **237 times** in six days and counted zero times (`telemetry/gate_blocks.go:54-58`, 0 callers).

- Input: `GetSessionDayActivity` (`store/position_query.go:125-155`) — `SUM(pnl_corrected)` over `status='CLOSED'` rows since `CMESessionDayStart`, excluding `reconcile_flat` / `unresolved` / `test_seam` close reasons, scoped to the active account. **Realized only.** An open position sitting −$300 counts $0.

- Effect on DEV: `SetDailyForceFlat` (`risk_limits.go:177`) → `EntryGate` leg D (`entry_gate.go:157-172`) refuses on both paths; the arm pass cancels an existing resting arm for a refused spec the same cycle (`armed_executor.go:510-522`). Cleared by `ResetDailyPnLAt` at the 17:00 CT roll (`risk_limits.go:216-222`).
- **Hole 1 — the arm window [B].** Loop order in `runCycle`: `maybeManageArmedOrders` at `auto_trader_loop.go:432` (reads the trip, cancels) → `skipWhileOpen` at `:475` → `GetFullDecisionWithStrategy` at `:550` (sets the trip). A loss closes at T crossing −450. Cycle 1 (≤2 min later): the arm pass reads no trip; the AI cycle sets it. Cycle 2: the arm pass refuses and cancels. A resting limit can fill anywhere in that **up-to-~4-minute** window, and after a stop-out the tape is often exactly where the next arm sits.
- **Hole 2 — realized-only.** With one-open-position the worst realised day is bounded at roughly −450 minus one full stop: at the p90 arm stop (59 pts, $118) ≈ **−$568**; at the 150-pt maximum arm stop in the ledger ($300) ≈ **−$750** (q17). That is acceptable at one lot; it doubles at two.

Within-day worst running point: median −$160, three days below −$300. **$450 is right for a 1-lot** — it is above the ordinary bad day (−$160 to −$300) and below the day that means something is wrong.

## Q5 — CORRELATION AND REGIME: WHAT KILLS ONE INSTRUMENT, ONE STRATEGY

**What the system has seen [T] (q11):** 13 session-days, 2026-08-18 → 09-03, at daily ATR14 395–490 pts (1.33–1.66% of price), daily ranges 242–569 pts (0.82–1.95%). 2026 is already the widest year in the 1,898-day history (median daily range 475 pts vs 351 in 2025, 266 all-time) — and the sample sits BELOW 2026's own median ATR (410 vs 529). So the tape the cage was tuned on is a calm stretch inside a loud year.

**What it has not seen:** 42.9% of all MNQ trading days since 2019-05 had a higher ATR14% than the sample's maximum; 28.9% had a wider daily range% than the sample's widest day; **111 days ran above 2× the sample's median ATR%** (2.78%) — 34 in 2020, 47 in 2022, 6 in 2024, 20 in 2025, 4 in 2026 (last 2026-06-25). The all-time worst day was 2026-04-08 at 12.94% range (2,653 pts); a 1.5×ATR5m stop on that tape is not a stop, it is a hope.

**What kills it, in the order I would expect [I], and what the system knows today [A]:**

1. **A vol regime shift.** [R] Lo's adaptive-markets argument and the 2012–2015 decay I watched are the same lesson: the level-reaction statistics this system trades (touch → HOLD/BREAK in `touch_outcomes`, n=677) are regime-conditional; a system tuned at 1.4% ATR does not know what a 3% tape does to its stop floor, its R:R at fill, or its fill quality. **The system computes the regime and does nothing with it:** `kernel/regime.go:35-37, 69-76` buckets `ATRRegime` LOW/NORMAL/HIGH/EXTREME by percentile of the daily series; the only consumer is a prompt string (`auto_trader_planner.go:2457`). **Refuse new entries when daily ATR14% > 2× the strict-era median** (today 2.78%), and reduce the per-trade cap proportionally between 1.5× and 2×. Data first: log the ATR% bucket on every trade so that in 60 days you can measure whether the edge, if any, survives HIGH.
2. **Roll week (SEP26 → DEC26).** SEP26 expires Friday 2026-09-18 08:30 CT (contract_roll.go header, CME rulebook cite). `VLContractResolver.cs:55` `RollDaysBeforeExpiry = 8` → the AddOn resolves DEC26 from **Thursday 2026-09-10**. The resolution happens per order (`VLTraderTCPClient.cs:901`) and at BarsRequest creation (`:1093`); BarsRequests are rebuilt only on feed drop/reconnect (`:205-373`) [A]. So from 09-10 until the next NT8 reconnect, **orders route to DEC26 while the bar stream — and every level cut from it (PDH/PDL/PDC, ONH/ONL, VWAP, IB, OR) — is SEP26** [B]. The Sep/Dec basis on MNQ is a positive carry of roughly [I] 100–250 points at current rates. A "reject at PDL" arm placed on DEC26 at a SEP26 price is a limit order 100–250 points inside the market — it fills on touch at the wrong level or never. The Go roll gate (`trader/contract_roll.go`, 3 days before the RESOLVED contract's expiry) is a backstop that after 09-10 keys on DEC26 (expiry 12-18) and so never covers SEP26's last week; the Go `DaysUntilExpiry` warning (`tcp_trader.go:358-360`) fires at ≤5 days on the bare root and warns, not refuses. No back-adjust or basis code exists (grep, q-appendix). **What I would refuse:** no new entries on session-day 09-10 (Wed 17:00 → Thu 14:45) and none on 09-11 until a human has confirmed in the log that the bars ACK carries "MNQ 12-26" and PDC/PDH/PDL were cut from DEC26 bars; force a BarsRequest rebuild (NT8 restart is the documented way) before the 09-10 ASIA read. Owner ruling required; it is a calendar item, not a code wave.
3. **FOMC and the red-news tape.** 2026-09-16 FOMC statement 13:00 CT / presser 13:30 CT is in `calendar_slices` — as the **static fallback** (`source='static'`, q13b), because the forexfactory feed's last real slice is 2026-09-04 (NFP). The T1 force-flat (`auto_trader_clock.go:673-703`) will cancel arms and flatten at T-lead before it, fail-closed, and the slice-missing P0 alert will fire on 09-08 and 09-09 (no slice at all for those dates). That is the right behaviour; the thing to verify before 09-16 is that the static times are the real times (they are: 18:00Z / 18:30Z). [I] What I refuse beyond the window: no NEW entries from the FOMC statement to the cash close — the post-presser tape reverses its first move more often than not and every seated level is stale.
4. **A trend day against the fade book.** The book is 42 shorts / 23 longs; longs run −$38/trade with a CI that excludes zero ([−69.6, −6.4], t=−2.36, q10), on a window where the index net drifted only −54 pts (29,586 → 29,532, q15) — so it is not a trend that explains it. ASIA is the other bucket with a CI below zero (n=16, mean −34.5, [−60.6, −8.4], t=−2.59; p(win) 0.133 Wilson [0.037, 0.379]). **Both are subject to the multiple-comparison caveat** — I ran ~12 buckets; at α=0.05 I expect ~0.6 false positives — so neither is a rule change yet. They are the two buckets I would put on the screen and re-test at n=30 strict-era each before touching anything.
5. **Correlation.** One instrument, one strategy, one broker feed, one model. The correlation that matters is not MNQ vs ES; it is that **every trade shares the same three failure modes** — the NT8 feed (feed_status latch, memory), the planner (17/17 fail-closes model-output), and the level engine (roll basis). A weekly limit is the only diversification available at this size: it caps how many days of one bad mode you eat before a human looks.

## ONE-PAGE SUMMARY

**Verdict for this section: the monitoring surface is well built where it exists and structurally blind where it matters. It cannot tell the owner the three things a desk pays a clerk to say — "the bot is dead", "the feed is dead", and "you are down $X against a limit that is off".** Two consecutive trading afternoons proved it: 09-03 (113 min 51 s of silence, then ~48 min alive-but-blind) and — this is new, found in the store today — **09-04, when the bar feed died at 12:19 CT and stayed dead through the 16:00 close (FEED DOWN logged every minute 12:30:01→15:59:35, 347 `cycle_skip=no_new_data` lines vs 29–41 on a normal day), the 13:25:47 boot came up INTO that dead feed with `🚨 FEED DOWN: no NT8 bar for 1h6m47s` in its own boot block, and was accepted as a passed boot.** [A] Both days the in-app P0 fired (14:28:39 on 09-03, 12:30:01 on 09-04) and neither was acked. The bell currently shows **420 unacked** alerts; a P0 has nowhere to stand out.

**Three biggest problems**

1. **Liveness is watched only by the thing that dies.** The alert bus (`trader/auto_trader_alerts.go:15 emitAlert` → `day_plan_alerts` → AlertCenter) and the UI itself (Go serves `web/dist`) are in-process. The AddOn — on Windows, which survived both outages — has **no NT8 `Alert()`/sound anywhere** (`grep -E "Alert\(|PlaySound" ninjascript/*.cs` → none; it logs only `LogLevel.Information|Warning`). The one bot-independent watcher on the host, `nofx-clock-guard.timer` (every 15 min, proven pattern, powershell interop working), watches the clock and nothing else. And the connect/disconnect events the Go side does log (`tcp_server: hello handshake OK`, `provider/ninjatrader/tcp_server.go:1713`) go to `slog.Default()` → journald, **never to `nofx_*.log`** (0 occurrences in four log files), where journald currently retains 16.5 hours because `tcp_server` logs every heartbeat/snapshot/balance frame at INFO (908 of 1,429 journal lines in the last hour, on a closed Saturday) — the class-12 probe, re-run, FAILS.
2. **The dashboard shows no risk number a trader would act on, and the one endpoint that has any lies.** `/api/risk/status` reports `daily_loss_limit_usd: 500`, `kill_switch_armed: true`, `last_reset_utc: <now>` and `daily_pnl_usd` = equity − initial (all-time, not daily) — while the bound strategy `MNQ` carries `guardrails_enabled=false`, `daily_loss_limit_usd=450`, `daily_loss_enabled=false` (`q26b`). Nothing in `web/src` reads that endpoint anyway. Open risk in $, distance to stop in ticks, session P&L vs limit, arm age, broker-vs-ledger, last-fill slippage, next planner read, day range vs ATR: none is on screen (Q2 has the mock).
3. **Two owner-facing records lie by construction.** (a) Every LONDON and NY *session digest* is written at **00:00:56 CT — before the session runs** (`day_plan_digests` ids 55/56/59/60/63/64, `q33`), and `SaveIfAbsent` means the real close can never overwrite it: "NY 2026-09-02 — 0 entries · realized +0.0 · flat" against two NY entries in the store that day (ids 589, 590, −254.0). That text seeds the next planner read (`auto_trader_planner.go:2253`). (b) `planner_read_facts` carries `version=0`, `tokens_in=0`, `bias_ai=''`, `bias_tree=''` on **32 of 32** rows (`q43`) — four instrument columns that have never been written.

**Three biggest opportunities**

1. **A two-sided dead-man that survives the bot** — built from parts that already exist: (i) the AddOn's own 30 s heartbeat/ack loop + a 5 s reconnect loop (`VLTraderTCPClient.cs:38-43, 525-580`) → add an NT8-side alert (log at Alert level + sound) when the Go ack is absent >90 s during CME open OR when NT8 is `Connected` but no bar has reached the AddOn in 3 min (the 09-04 class); (ii) a `nofx-liveness.timer` cloned from `nofx-clock-guard` (every 2 min in CME hours; checks `/api/health`, newest 1m bar age, newest `decision_records` age, `no_new_data` rate) writing a state JSON the boot block reads. Neither is a new channel: NT8 and the user timers are already on the desk. What it takes: C# + shell + one owner ruling on sound.
2. **A DESK STRIP above the plan card** (Q2 mock) fed entirely from functions that already exist: `GetSessionDayActivity` (P&L on `pnl_corrected`), the class-33 leg-4 join (broker book vs ledger, `nt8_order_snapshots` age 12 s right now), `plan/today.reading/replan_in_flight`, the session registry's next scheduled read, `LoadFeedPolicy`, `bars` for range/ATR. Ten numbers, one row each, every one with an as-of.
3. **An honest EOD page** (Q5) — seven queries, all runnable today against the store, that replace the digest's "0 entries · flat" with the day's closes on `pnl_corrected`, arms placed/filled/cancelled by reason, silent refusals from `decision_records.execution_log`, feed gaps from `decision_records`/`bars`, touches recorded, planner reads with their facts, and the boots that happened.
